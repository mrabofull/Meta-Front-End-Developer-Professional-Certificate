Why Study Space?

Understanding space helps us to unlock the secrets of our universe. By studying planets, stars, and galaxies, we gain insights that can improve life on Earth, help us understand our place in the universe, and fuel our curiosity about the unknown.

The Fascinating Solar System

Our solar system consists of the Sun, eight planets, and numerous moons, comets, and asteroids. Discovering how these celestial bodies interact gives us a better understanding of space physics and the potential for life beyond Earth.
